STORMWATER 101
When rain falls on a natural catchment, much of it soaks into the soil.
Build roofs, roads and car parks, and that water has nowhere to go.
It rushes into the drains all at once, and the flood peak shoots up.
A detention basin catches that rush, and lets it out slowly through a small outlet.
The peak drops, and it arrives later, giving the creek downstream time to cope.
